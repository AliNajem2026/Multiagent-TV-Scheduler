Programming Day Parts:

00:00 - 06:00  Overnight / Late Night
  - Use archive sports replays and classic matches
  - Minimise cost — avoid premium live content
  - 30-60 minute segments acceptable
  - Repeat programmes from earlier in the day

06:00 - 09:00  Breakfast
  - Lead with Morning News at 06:00
  - Follow with Sports Roundup and headlines
  - Keep segments short (30-60 min)

09:00 - 12:00  Mid-Morning
  - Documentaries, Sports Science, Magazine shows
  - Build audience toward afternoon
  - 60 minute segments

12:00 - 18:00  Afternoon
  - Sports highlights and analysis
  - Live sports if available (especially 15:00-18:00)
  - News update at 12:00 and 16:00
  - Longer programmes (60-90 min) in late afternoon

18:00 - 23:00  Prime Time
  - Live sports events first priority
  - Premium sports analysis after live events
  - Avoid placing documentaries here unless no live sport
  - Signature programmes 19:00-22:00

23:00 - 00:00  Late Evening
  - News wrap and post-match review
  - Transition to overnight content
  - 30-60 minute segments

General Rules:
- Avoid same category more than 3 times consecutively
- News should appear at 06:00, 12:00, 18:00, and 23:00 minimum
- Sports analysis should immediately follow live matches
- Overnight can repeat programmes from earlier in the day
- High priority content goes in prime time
- Schedule must cover all 24 hours without gaps
